House story

We borrowed a professional layout and filled it with crumbs.

Qpears is a small-goods studio that looks like it belongs on a quiet shopping street and behaves like it was founded during dessert. The template is composed: serif headlines, generous space, a serious footer. The products are a seed, a sticker, a sock, and a crumb with a SKU.

Checkout runs through Square because someone has to be the adult. That someone is software.

The name

Qpears is “cute pears” if you are late, and “questionable pears” if you are honest.

The look

Editorial shop. Olive, cream, and the kind of spacing that implies we own a linen table.

The bit

We sell small things because shipping a sofa is less funny and also harder to lose in a coat.

Qpears

Founded in a group chat. Styled like a gallery. Stocked like a joke shop.
